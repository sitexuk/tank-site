Privacy policy

Your rides stay on your iPhone.

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short
  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no advertising.
  • Your profile, products, plans, routes and rides are stored only on your iPhone.
  • We (the developer) never receive any of your data.
  • One optional exception: if a route file has no elevation and you choose to look it up, the route’s coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo. Nothing else is sent.

Who we are

Tank is made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Questions about this policy: support.tank@icloud.com.

What Tank stores, and where

To build your plans, Tank keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your profile: body weight, units, how you fuel today, gut tolerance, whether you’re a salty sweater, your usual road pace, optional age group, and adjustments learned from your rides.
  • Your fuel library: the products you add and their carbs and sodium.
  • Routes: GPX files you open are read on your iPhone. Tank keeps the analysed route (distance, elevation, climbs, any refill stops) with plans you save.
  • Rides and check-ins: what you ate, how the ride went and how long it took.

This data isn’t uploaded to us or to anyone else. It’s included in your normal iPhone backup if you use one. Deleting the app deletes it.

The optional elevation lookup

Some route files contain no elevation. In that case Tank offers to look it up, and only does so if you tap to agree. It then sends the route’s latitude and longitude points to Open-Meteo, a free weather and elevation service, and receives the elevation for each point. No name, account, device identifier or other personal information is sent with them. Open-Meteo’s own privacy terms apply to that request. If you don’t agree, nothing is sent and your plan still works, just without climbs.

Notifications

In-ride nudges are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They don’t pass through any server.

Purchases

Tank Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We don’t see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active subscription.

Analytics and crash reports

Tank contains no analytics or advertising code. If you’ve chosen to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Tank isn’t aimed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it completely: edit it in the app, delete individual plans and rides, or delete the app to remove everything. Under UK GDPR you can contact us at any time with questions.

Health information

Tank isn’t medical advice and isn’t a medical device. Its suggestions are based on published sports-nutrition guidance for healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, check with a professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
