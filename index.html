The plan

Your numbers, with the reason

Tell Tank how long, how hard and how hot. Your weight, gut and how salty you sweat do the rest. Every number comes with a one-line reason, so you know why it’s 60 and not 90.

  • Carbs, fluid and sodium per hour, and for the whole ride
  • A carb-based meal to eat before you start
  • Comfortable limits while your gut is still learning
Your route

Drop in a GPX. We’ll find the climbs.

Share a route from Strava, Komoot, Ride with GPS or Garmin. Tank smooths out GPS noise, finds the real climbs and estimates your time from your own pace.

  • Road, mixed, gravel or MTB: each changes your time and your fuel
  • Mark café stops and check your bottles cover every leg
  • Learns your real speed after every ride
The timeline

Food moved to just before the climb

Every gel, bar, bottle and refill on the clock and on the map. Nothing to eat on the first part of a climb; drink as normal.

  • km markers on every item, to match your bike computer
  • A pack list built from the products you actually use
  • A plan check: every total within 5% of target
On the bike

Nudges that reach you, phone locked

A countdown you can read at a glance, and big Done and Skip buttons. Tap Done right from the lock screen or your watch.

  • A reminder to drink every 20 minutes, sized to what you need
  • Ate early? The rest of the plan moves with you

  • Eaten so far vs due by now, at a glance
Your fuel

Plans built from your packets

Add the gels, bars, chews and drink mixes you ride with, straight from the label. Sweets by the bag? Enter them per 100 g. Switch things off for today and the plan adapts.

“Gummy bears (25 g): 19 g carbs per serving.”
Grounded, not guessed

Built on published sports-nutrition guidance

Every number in Tank traces back to research, and gets smarter with every ride you log.

Carbs by duration

About 30 g an hour for 1–2 hours, up to 90 on long days. Limited by your gut, not your weight.

Jeukendrup 2014 · ACSM 2016

Fluid from your sweat

Bigger riders and hotter days need more. Tank keeps you within about 2% of your body weight, and never plans more than you sweat.

ACSM 2007 · Baker 2016

Learns from your rides

A quick check-in after each ride tunes your carbs and your speed. Bonked, bloated, flying? The next plan knows.

Gut training: Jeukendrup 2017

Tank Pro · route intelligence

Reads your route like a rider

Drop in a GPX from Komoot, Strava or Ride with GPS. Tank works out where the ride gets hard, and plans your fuel around it.

Finds every climb

Long drags and short, steep kicks, even when a flat stretch splits them up.

Times it from the gradients

Slower up, faster down, tuned to your own speed. Your ride time sets how much you need.

Food off the climbs

Nothing to eat when you’re gasping: gels move to just before or after, while drinks stay where they are.

Hilly means harder

A lumpy route plans more carbs than a flat one at the same pace, because the climbs burn through them faster.
