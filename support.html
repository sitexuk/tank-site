Plans and numbers

Why doesn’t my weight change the carbs per hour?
How many carbs you can use per hour is limited by how fast your gut absorbs sugar, and research shows that isn’t linked to body weight. Your weight does change your fluid, sodium and the meal before your ride. Each plan shows a one-line reason under the carbs number.
Why is my plan capped at 60 g an hour?
Above about 60 g an hour you need products that mix glucose and fructose, and a gut that’s used to it. If you regularly handle more, switch Profile › Your gut to “Trained gut”.
Does Tank learn from my rides?
Yes. After each ride, the check-in (energy, stomach, what you ate, and for routes how long it took) adjusts your future carbs and your pace. You can see and reset what it has learned in Profile.
Is this medical advice?
No. Tank is based on published sports-nutrition guidance for healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, check with a professional.

Routes

Where do I get a GPX file?
In Strava (on the website), Komoot, Ride with GPS or Garmin Connect, open the route or a past ride and choose Export GPX. On your iPhone, use Share › Tank, or choose the file in Tank › Upload route. Many events also offer the course as a GPX download.
The ride time looks wrong
Pick the right surface (road, mixed, gravel or MTB) and set your usual road pace in Profile. You can also set your own time with the + and − buttons. After route rides, the check-in learns your real speed.
Why are there no climbs on my route?
Some route files have no elevation. Tank offers to look it up online (only if you agree; see the privacy policy). Very gentle routes may simply have no climbs worth planning around.

On the bike

I’m not getting nudges with my phone locked
Check Settings › Notifications › Tank is on, with Time Sensitive Notifications allowed. If you use a Focus while riding, allow Tank in that Focus. Nudges are scheduled when you tap Start ride.
I ate earlier or later than planned
Tap Done when you eat. If that’s more than a few minutes off the plan, the rest of your food and drink moves with you. Running behind on a route? Tap “I’m behind”.

Subscriptions

What’s free and what’s Pro?
Free: quick plans, the timeline, your fuel library, your first route plan and your first ride with nudges. Pro adds every route, nudges on every ride, saved plans and plans that learn from your rides.
How do I cancel?
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Tank Pro, or Tank › Profile › Manage subscription. Cancel at least a day before renewal. Your saved plans and rides stay readable.
I changed phone. How do I get Pro back?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Profile › Restore purchases.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
